- Email-based login with mandatory email verification
- Registration with email, username, and password
- Custom account adapter to generate confirmation URLs pointing to the React frontend
- Custom verify_email view to bypass dj-rest-auth's broken VerifyEmailView in allauth 65
- File-based email backend for development to avoid quoted-printable URL corruption
